Privacy Policy

Pixel Postcards: Nonogram

Effective date: 17 October 2026

In short: Pixel Postcards has no accounts and collects no personal information directly. Your puzzles, progress and settings stay on your device. Because the app shows ads and offers in-app purchases and subscriptions, Google and RevenueCat process some data, as explained below.

1. Who we are

Pixel Postcards is developed by Emre Özgöz. Contact: emreozgoz@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

Kept only in the app's own storage on your device; never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

  • Puzzles finished and puzzles in progress
  • Gold balance, daily streak
  • Settings (language, appearance, haptics, mistake checking)
  • Which purchases and subscriptions are active (a cached copy)

4. Tracking and ad choices

On iPhone, the app asks for permission before using your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised with that identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland a consent form is shown first; you can revisit it in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Buying No Ads, All Albums or the Puzzle Pass removes the ads between puzzles.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child's data was processed, contact us and we will act on it.

6. Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. Uninstalling the app removes all game data. To have purchase records held by RevenueCat deleted, email us; we answer within 30 days. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings.

7. Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above.
