Pixel Postcards

Colour nonogram puzzles for iPhone

Questions, a puzzle that seems wrong, or a purchase that did not arrive? Email emreozgoz@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

How to play

The numbers beside each row and above each column list the coloured runs in that line, in order. Runs of the same colour have at least one empty cell between them; different colours may touch. Pick a colour, tap or drag to paint, and use ✕ to mark cells you know are empty. Every postcard can be solved with logic alone.

Purchases and subscriptions

  • Restore purchases: Shop → Restore purchases, or Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Puzzle Pass: renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
