Found a problem or have an idea? Write to me: emreozgoz@gmail.com. I usually reply within 2 business days.

My purchase is missing

Go to Settings → Restore purchases. Chapters, Permanent 2x Income and the Starter Pack come back with the same Apple Account. Token packs are consumable and are not restored.

How do I cancel the Conductor Card?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Next Stop. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to stop renewal.

My progress was reset

Your save is stored only on your device; deleting the app deletes it. Purchases come back with "Restore purchases".
