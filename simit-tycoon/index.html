Simit Tycoon

Tezgâhta simit satarak başla, çırak tut, fırın aç, pastaneden şube zincirine ve dünya şubelerine uzan. Sen yokken de çalışanların satmaya devam eder.

Start by selling simit at a street stall, hire apprentices, open a bakery and grow into a chain with shops around the world. Your staff keep selling while you are away.
